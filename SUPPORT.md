# Support policy

DecAustrum is published as a source-available portfolio evaluation project. It is
not a hosted service and no support plan is included.

The owner does not undertake to provide:

- installation or deployment assistance;
- answers to issues or questions;
- bug or security fixes;
- maintenance, updates, compatibility work, or new features;
- availability, response-time, or service-level commitments; or
- production suitability or security certification.

Public issues, discussions, or messages do not create a support relationship or
an obligation to respond. Do not place credentials, personal data, customer
information, or undisclosed vulnerability details in a public issue.

Suspected vulnerabilities must be handled through the private process described
in the [security policy](.github/SECURITY.md). Receiving a report does not create
a promise to investigate, remediate, publish, or respond within a particular
period.

Support, hosting, maintenance, production rights, or service commitments are
available only if agreed separately in writing. The full warranty and liability
terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
